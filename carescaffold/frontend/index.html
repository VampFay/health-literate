PHI redaction

Your personal health info (names, phone, MRN, etc.) is stripped and replaced with [TOKEN] before any AI call. Your data never leaves your device in its original form.

Outbound safety check (2 layers)

Every AI response is checked by (1) regex patterns for dosage/diagnosis/directive language, then (2) an independent AI judge. If either layer flags the response, you see a care-team redirect instead.

📋 Honest Compliance Disclosure

  • Not "HIPAA certified" — no such certification exists for software. PHI redaction is built and tested against the HIPAA Safe Harbor standard (45 CFR 164.514(b)(2)); 90.86% recall / 85.24% precision on a 152-case adversarial corpus.
  • Not "ONC certified" — the FHIR R4 endpoint is self-tested against the R4 spec; Inferno's Docker conformance suite was not run (Docker unavailable in sandbox).
  • 100% synthetic data — all patient data is generated by Synthea (MITRE's open-source synthetic patient generator). This system has never touched real PHI.
  • BAA not required — because no real PHI is processed, a Business Associate Agreement with the LLM/embedding providers is not required for this demo. A production version would require one.
